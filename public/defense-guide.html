1 Act 1: Opening & Theoretical Framing (2 Mins)
Presenter 1 (Opening Spiel): "Good day to our esteemed panel members: Dr. Jayrelle Usy, Dr. Giovanni Yen, and our Chairman, Engr./Prof. Mark Jarus Talaquines. We are the proponents of SubSync: An Automated Web-Based Platform for Enhancing Community Engagement in Residential Subdivisions.

Traditional homeowners associations suffer from fragmented paper records, unverified announcements, delayed complaint blotters, and opaque finances. SubSync digitalizes subdivision operations around four core specific objectives:
1. Integrated Communication with draft-to-publish notices and formal messaging.
2. Automated Issue Reporting with geofenced location-pinning, rule-based auto-priority, and immutable audit logs.
3. Community Recommendations empowering civic participatory governance.
4. Dynamic Geospatial Mapping using Leaflet to visualize facilities, occupancy, and geofenced incidents.
Allow us to present the live start-to-finish workflow."

❓ Anticipated Panel Q&A
Q: "Why are there two separate financial ledgers (Personal Balance vs. HOA Expenses)?"
Answer: "SubSync intentionally separates individual resident accounting from community fiscal transparency. The personal ledger tracks private resident obligations (monthly dues and GCash receipts). The HOA Expenses ledger publicly discloses how association dues are utilized (security payroll, pool chemicals, street lighting) with verifiable PDF receipts, satisfying civic transparency."
Q: "How does the system protect against Insecure Direct Object Reference (IDOR) on household members?"
Answer: "In ResidentController::authorizeMember(), resident requests are strictly scoped to their authenticated house_id. Any attempt by a resident to read, modify, or delete family members belonging to another household returns an immediate 403 Forbidden."
Q: "How do you avoid session collisions when Admin and Resident open tabs on the same computer?"
Answer: "SubSync employs Laravel multi-guard authentication (admin, officer, and resident guards). Custom session cookies (subsync_admin_session, subsync_resident_session) are resolved by our global ResolvePortalSession middleware before session initialization, preventing privilege escalation or accidental session overwrite."
Q: "What happens if WebSockets fail or are blocked by carrier firewalls?"
Answer: "SubSync uses a dual-layer real-time architecture: it is WebSocket-first using Laravel Reverb for low-overhead push, but automatically downgrades to an HTTP-based Server-Sent Events (SSE) stream (/api/stream/updates) if WebSocket handshakes fail."
